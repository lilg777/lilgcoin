Sell LGX

Swap LGX for BNB right here — connect your wallet and trade on PancakeSwap, without leaving lilgcoin.org.

If the swap doesn't load, open it on PancakeSwap instead.

How it works

  1. Connect the wallet that holds your LGX.
  2. Enter how much LGX to sell — the swap shows you how much BNB you'll get.
  3. Check the rate, confirm in your wallet, and the BNB lands in the same wallet.

Good to know

  • The swap runs on PancakeSwap. Your wallet connects directly to them — lilgcoin.org never holds your funds.
  • Check the rate before you confirm. The main LGX pool is still small, so a large sale can move the price — the swap shows you the price impact before you confirm.
  • You'll need a little BNB in the wallet to pay the transaction fee.
  • Want cash out after that? Sell the BNB back to dollars through an off-ramp such as MoonPay.
