LiLGcoin for Individuals

Peer-to-peer money you control — no bank in the middle.

Get a wallet

Get a wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain, for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask. Add a small amount of BNB to the wallet to pay for transaction fees (gas). Your wallet holds your LGX — nobody else controls it. Not sure which one? See Choose your wallet.

Get LGX

Swap BNB for LGX on PancakeSwap using the LGX contract address:

0xbec041bc90cae81596df063c3eee4e858fc3903d

Always verify the address on BscScan before you swap.

Use LGX

  • Send it anywhere — LGX works worldwide, with no borders and no bank holidays.
  • Fast, low-fee payments — built for cheaper transactions and everyday use.
  • Hold it — supply is fixed at 7.5 million LGX, with no hidden inflation.
  • Earn yields — become a liquidity provider on eligible exchanges and earn yields from LiLGcoin transaction fees.

Keep it safe

  • Only use the contract address above, verified on BscScan.
  • Never share your wallet's seed phrase or private keys with anyone.
  • Back up your wallet before you move any funds.
