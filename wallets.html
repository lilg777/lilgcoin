Choose your wallet

Pick a wallet that holds LGX on Binance Smart Chain — mobile for everyday use, desktop for full control, or hardware for the most security.

Mobile wallets

Portable and convenient — your LGX is with you wherever you go, and you can scan QR codes to pay face-to-face. If you lose your phone without a backup, you can lose your funds.

Trust WalletGood for beginners

Mobile wallet for iOS and Android with Binance Smart Chain built in. Add LGX with the contract address below, and use its built-in browser to swap on PancakeSwap.

MetaMask

Mobile app for iOS and Android. Add Binance Smart Chain as a network, then add LGX with the contract address below and connect to PancakeSwap.

SafePal

Mobile app for iOS and Android with Binance Smart Chain built in. Add LGX with the contract address below, swap on PancakeSwap through its built-in dApp browser, and pair it with the SafePal S1 hardware wallet later if you want to upgrade security.

MathWallet

Multi-chain wallet for iOS, Android, and the web with Binance Smart Chain built in. Add LGX with the contract address below and use its built-in dApp store to reach PancakeSwap.

Desktop wallets

Full control over your funds on your own computer. Some desktop wallets can also pair with a hardware wallet. Watch out for malware — keep your computer clean and your seed phrase offline.

MetaMaskMost used

Browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and more, plus a mobile app. Add Binance Smart Chain as a custom network, add LGX with the contract address, and connect to PancakeSwap in one click.

Hardware wallets

The most secure way to store funds — your keys stay on the device, offline. Best for larger amounts you don't need every day. If you lose the device without a backup, your funds are unrecoverable.

Ledger

Hardware wallet that works with Binance Smart Chain through MetaMask. Keep your LGX offline and only connect when you need to send.

Trezor

Hardware wallet that works with Binance Smart Chain through MetaMask. Same idea — keys stay on the device, off the internet.

Coming next

LiLGcoin plans to offer a specific cold wallet made for LGX and other crypto assets, plus an LGX debit/credit card for spending.

How to add LGX to your wallet

  1. Open your wallet and make sure Binance Smart Chain is selected (add it as a network in MetaMask if it isn't there).
  2. Add a custom token and paste the LGX contract address: 0xbec041bc90cae81596df063c3eee4e858fc3903d
  3. Verify the address on BscScan first — only use this address.
  4. Add a small amount of BNB to the wallet to pay for transaction fees (gas), then swap BNB for LGX on PancakeSwap.

Keep it safe

  • Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone.
  • Back up your wallet before you move any funds.
  • Only use the contract address above, verified on BscScan.
