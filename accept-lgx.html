Accept LiLGcoin (LGX)

A step-by-step guide for merchants — take LGX payments from your customers, with cheaper transactions and faster confirmations.

Why accept LGX

  • Cheaper transactions — LiLGcoin was designed for cheaper transactions and to be more efficient for everyday use.
  • Faster confirmations — merchants get faster confirmation times, while still having the ability to wait for more confirmations when selling bigger ticket items.
  • Peer-to-peer — payments go directly between you and your customer, with no central authority or banks.
  • Worldwide customers — LGX works anywhere in the world, so you can sell beyond your local market.

What you need

  • A wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain, for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask.
  • A small amount of BNB in the wallet to pay for transaction fees (gas).
  • Your wallet address — the one customers send LGX to.

The steps

Step 1 — Get a wallet

Install Trust Wallet or MetaMask, create a wallet, and back up your seed phrase. Never share your seed phrase or private keys with anyone.

Step 2 — Show customers where to pay

Display your wallet address at checkout — on your website, at your counter, or as a QR code. The customer sends LGX from their own wallet to that address.

Step 3 — Price in your local currency

Display prices in your local currency and accept LGX at the current rate. Check the current LGX price on Coinbrain or GeckoTerminal when you take a payment.

Step 4 — Confirm the payment

Every payment can be verified on the public blockchain. Look it up on BscScan using the LGX contract address 0xbec041bc90cae81596df063c3eee4e858fc3903d. For bigger ticket items, wait for more confirmations before handing over the goods.

Step 5 — Keep records

Save each transaction's record — date, amount in LGX, and the rate you used. For guidance on tax compliance in your own jurisdiction, contact a qualified accountant.

Good to know

  • Payments are final — once an LGX payment is confirmed on the blockchain, it cannot be reversed.
  • You control the funds — LGX goes straight to your wallet. No bank holds it, and no one else can freeze it.
  • Low fees — transaction fees on Binance Smart Chain are small, so more of each sale stays with you.

Earn more with LGX

Using eligible exchanges, merchants can earn yields from LiLGcoin transaction fees by becoming a liquidity provider. And more is coming: an LGX debit/credit card for spending, the LGX Crypto Exchange, and LGX Gaming.

Let people know you accept LGX

Tell your customers — on your website, your storefront, and your social pages. A growing number of people hold LGX and look for places to spend it. Questions about accepting LGX? Email lilgcoinx@gmail.com.
