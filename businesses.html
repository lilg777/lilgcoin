LiLGcoin for Businesses

Accept LGX from your customers — cheaper transactions, faster confirmations, no bank in the middle.

Why accept LGX

  • Cheaper transactions — LiLGcoin was designed for cheaper transactions and to be more efficient for everyday use.
  • Faster confirmations — merchants get faster confirmation times, while still having the ability to wait for more confirmations when selling bigger ticket items.
  • Peer-to-peer — payments go directly between you and your customer, with no central authority or banks.
  • Worldwide customers — LGX works anywhere in the world, so you can sell beyond your local market.

How to accept LGX

  1. Get a wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain (for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask).
  2. Give customers your wallet address or a payment link, and they send LGX from their own wallet.
  3. Display prices in your local currency and accept LGX at the current rate — every payment can be verified on the public blockchain.

Step-by-step guide: How to accept LGX.

Reach LGX holders

Let people know you accept LGX — on your website, your storefront, and your social pages. A growing number of people hold LGX and look for places to spend it.

Earn yields

Using eligible exchanges, merchants can earn yields from LiLGcoin transaction fees by becoming a liquidity provider using whichever DeFi project they like.

Coming next

LiLGcoin plans to offer a specific cold wallet made for LGX and other crypto assets, an LGX debit/credit card for spending, the LGX Crypto Exchange, and LGX Gaming — more ways for merchants to use and earn LGX.

Taxes and accounting

For guidance on tax compliance in your own jurisdiction, contact a qualified accountant.
