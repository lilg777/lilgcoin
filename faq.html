Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is LiLGcoin?

LiLGcoin (LGX or ₲) is a peer-to-peer cryptocurrency and open-source software project on the Binance Smart Chain. It uses peer-to-peer technology to operate with no central authority or banks; managing transactions and the issuing of LGX is carried out collectively by the network. LiLGcoin is open-source; its design is public, nobody owns or controls LiLGcoin and everyone can take part.

Who created LiLGcoin?

LiLGcoin was created by LGXatoshi (Austin Douglas), proposed in 2020 and launched on October 30, 2021. It is the first crypto by a musician, inspired by Bitcoin, Dogecoin, Ethereum and Tron, and based on Wrapped Bitcoin's original source code.

How does LiLGcoin work?

LGX is a token running on the Binance Smart Chain. The network supports faster transactions than Bitcoin — Ethereum's average block time is about 15 seconds versus 10 minutes for Bitcoin — so merchants get faster confirmation times, while still having the ability to wait for more confirmations when selling bigger ticket items.

What is the total supply?

The total supply is 7.5 million LGX, with an initial supply of 750,000 coins at launch. Supply is fixed at launch, with no hidden inflation.

Is LiLGcoin a security?

LiLGcoin is designed to operate as a utility, not a speculative security.

Getting LGX

How do I buy LGX?

Get a wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain (for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask) and add some BNB for gas. Then open PancakeSwap and paste the LGX contract address to swap BNB for LGX.

What is the LGX contract address?

The LGX contract address on Binance Smart Chain is:

0xbec041bc90cae81596df063c3eee4e858fc3903d

Always verify the address on BscScan before you swap.

What wallet do I need?

Any wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain works — for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask. You will also need a small amount of BNB in the wallet to pay for transaction fees (gas).

Where can I see the price and liquidity?

Track LGX on BscScan, Coinbrain, GeckoTerminal and Bitquery.

What are the fees?

LiLGcoin was designed for cheaper transactions and low processing fees. You pay only the standard Binance Smart Chain network fee (gas, in BNB) when you send LGX or swap it.

Using LGX

Can I earn yields with LGX?

Using eligible exchanges, merchants can earn yields from LiLGcoin transaction fees by becoming a liquidity provider using whichever DeFi project they like. Liquidity pool fees remain in the pools to deepen liquidity.

What is the LGX Crypto Exchange?

A planned exchange where merchants can swap LiLGcoin for other DeFi projects and LGX Rare Gems, possibly worth 10-1000x more than LiLGcoin itself.

What about the LGX wallet and card?

LiLGcoin plans to be the first crypto to offer a specific cold wallet made for LGX and your other crypto assets, plus an LGX debit/credit card for spending in the future.

Can I earn LGX by gaming?

That is the plan for LGX Gaming: a game where merchants can earn LiLGcoin by doing daily tasks and playing casino games.
