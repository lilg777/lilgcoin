Community

LiLGcoin is open-source and community-run. There is no company behind LGX — the people who hold it, use it and build on it are the project. This page is the map of where the community lives.

Development and discussion

LiLGcoin is open-source. The code, the white paper and the project history live on GitHub — issues and contributions are welcome. Longer-form discussion runs on BitcoinTalk, where LiLGcoin was first announced in 2021.

Stay safe

Only trust links from this page. LiLGcoin admins will never message you first asking for money, and nobody from the project will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key — anyone who does is a scammer, whatever their profile says. If you are ever unsure whether an account is really ours, check it against the links here.
