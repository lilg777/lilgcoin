LiLGcoin is a peer-to-peer cryptocurrency and a new kind of money.

Get started with LiLGcoin

LiLGcoin (LGX or ₲) uses peer-to-peer technology to operate with no central authority or banks; managing transactions and the issuing of LGX is carried out collectively by the network. LiLGcoin is open-source; its design is public, nobody owns or controls LiLGcoin and everyone can take part. Through many of its unique properties, LiLGcoin allows exciting uses that could not be covered by any previous payment system. Proposed in 2020 by self-taught programmer Austin Douglas, the network went live with an initial supply of 750,000 coins and a total supply of 7.5 million coins on 30 October 2021 — aimed to link small businesses and big businesses together.

Get started with LiLGcoin

  1. Get a wallet that supports Binance Smart Chain (for example Trust Wallet or MetaMask) and add some BNB for gas. Not sure which one? See Choose your wallet.
  2. Open PancakeSwap and paste the LGX contract address below to swap BNB for LGX.
  3. Contract (BSC): 0xbec041bc90cae81596df063c3eee4e858fc3903d
  4. Buy LGX on PancakeSwap
