Glossary

New to crypto? This page explains, in plain English, the technical words used across lilgcoin.org. Tap any word to jump straight to it.

Address · BEP-20 · Binance Smart Chain · Blockchain · BNB · BscScan · Contract address · Cryptocurrency · DEX · Gas fees · Liquidity · Peer-to-peer · Private key · Seed phrase · Slippage · Smart contract · Stablecoin · Swap · Token · Wallet

Address

Your wallet’s public receiving address — a long string of letters and numbers that starts with 0x on Binance Smart Chain. It works like an account number: it is safe to share so people can send you LGX, because an address can receive money, never send it.

BEP-20

The standard that tokens on Binance Smart Chain follow — a shared rulebook that lets every wallet, exchange and app handle a token the same way. LGX is a BEP-20 token.

Binance Smart Chain (BSC)

The blockchain network LGX runs on. It is built for speed and low cost — most transfers settle in seconds and cost a fraction of a cent in fees. It is also called BNB Smart Chain.

Blockchain

A public record book of transactions, kept by thousands of computers around the world instead of one company. Once a transaction is written into it, nobody can erase or change it.

BNB

The main coin of Binance Smart Chain. BNB is used to pay gas fees, so you need a small amount of it in your wallet to send LGX or make swaps — a few cents’ worth is usually plenty.

BscScan

A free website (bscscan.com) where anyone can look up transactions, wallet balances and tokens on Binance Smart Chain. It is how you confirm a payment really went through — a public receipt book for the whole network.

Contract address

The unique ID of a token on the blockchain — no two tokens share one. Names and symbols can be copied by scammers; a contract address cannot. That is why you should always copy LGX’s address from this site and never from a screenshot or a stranger’s post.

Cryptocurrency

Digital money that runs on a blockchain instead of through banks. Anyone can use it without asking permission, and it can be sent anywhere in the world.

DEX (decentralized exchange)

A website or app that lets you trade one token for another directly from your own wallet — no account to open and no company holding your money. The swap tool on our Buy page is powered by one.

Gas fees

The small fee paid to the network every time you send or swap tokens. On Binance Smart Chain, gas is paid in BNB and usually costs less than a cent.

Liquidity

How easily a token can be bought or sold without its price jumping around. Deep liquidity in a trading pool means smooth trades; thin liquidity — like LGX has today — means even small trades can move the price.

Peer-to-peer (P2P)

Directly from one person to another, with no middleman in between. Sending LGX wallet-to-wallet is peer-to-peer: no bank approves the payment or takes a cut.

Private key

The secret code that proves a wallet is yours and allows money to be spent from it. Your wallet app guards it for you. Never share it — anyone who has it can take everything in the wallet, and nobody legitimate will ever ask for it.

Seed phrase

A list of 12 to 24 words your wallet gives you when you create it — the master backup of the whole wallet. Type those words into a new wallet and your money is back. Write them on paper, keep them somewhere safe, and never share them or type them into a website: that is the number one way people lose crypto. See Choose your wallet.

Slippage

The difference between the price you expect when you start a swap and the price you actually get, because the market moved while the trade went through. Thin markets have more slippage, which is why smaller trades get a better rate.

Smart contract

A program that lives on the blockchain and runs exactly as it was written — no company operates it and nobody can change the rules mid-game. LGX itself is a smart contract, and so are the trading pools where it is swapped.

Stablecoin

A token designed to hold a steady value, usually tied to the US dollar — USDT and USDC are the best known. One dollar of a stablecoin is meant to always be worth one dollar.

Swap

Trading one token for another — for example, BNB for LGX. On this site you can swap right on the Buy page; your wallet handles the rest.

Token

A coin-like asset created on an existing blockchain instead of on its own network. LGX is a token on Binance Smart Chain. People often say "coin" for both — the difference is only where it lives.

Wallet

The app that holds your crypto keys and lets you send and receive — Trust Wallet and MetaMask are examples. A wallet does not store coins inside it like a piggy bank: the coins live on the blockchain, and the wallet holds the keys that control them. See Choose your wallet.
