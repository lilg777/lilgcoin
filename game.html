LGX Coin Catch

Drag the wallet to catch falling LGX coins. Gold coins score — scam coins cost a heart. Catch 3 scams and it is game over.

Move with your finger, your mouse, or the ← → arrow keys. It speeds up as you go.

How to play

  • Gold coins marked ₲ — catch them: +10 points. The big ones are worth +50.
  • Scam coins (dark, with a skull) — dodge them. Each one you catch costs a heart, and 3 hearts end the game.
  • Missed LGX does not hurt you. Only scams do — just like real crypto.

It is the golden rule in game form: grab the real thing, dodge the scams. New here? The FAQ explains LiLGcoin, and the Community page is where the players hang out.
